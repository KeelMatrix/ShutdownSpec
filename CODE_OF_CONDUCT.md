# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone. We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions, giving and accepting constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults or personal attacks, harassment, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Unacceptable behavior in public project spaces may be reported through the GitHub issue tracker so maintainers can review and moderate it. Do not use the security contact for Code of Conduct matters.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
